# Notes

Human-only working notes. **AI agents are denied read access to this
directory** via `permissions.deny` in `.claude/settings.json`.

Versioned in git, so it's the right place for thinking you want to keep and
share — reading lists, half-formed ideas, meeting notes, drafts not ready to
become design docs.

For anything genuinely private or throwaway, use `local/` instead — that's
gitignored and never leaves your machine.
